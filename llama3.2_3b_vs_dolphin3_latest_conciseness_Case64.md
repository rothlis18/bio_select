1. **Hypergamy**: Females tend to prefer higher-status males, even if it means sacrificing lower-status males with more resources.
2. **Mate value**: Females assign higher value to males with higher status, wealth, and social status.
3. **Protection-resource trade-offs**: Females prioritize males with high status and resources, as they provide better protection and resources for offspring.
